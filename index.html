About

I am an undergraduate in Transport Management and Logistics Engineering at the University of Moratuwa with a strong passion for the maritime, aviation, and logistics industries. I am particularly interested in improving transport systems through innovation, data-driven decision-making, and sustainable engineering solutions.

My academic background has strengthened my analytical and problem-solving skills, while my leadership experience as the Club Service Avenue Director of the Rotaract Club of the University of Moratuwa has enhanced my abilities in project management, teamwork, and communication. Through organizing and leading multiple large-scale projects and community initiatives, I have developed the confidence to work effectively in dynamic and collaborative environments.

I am committed to continuous learning and enjoy exploring emerging technologies, supply chain optimization, and smart mobility solutions that shape the future of global transportation. My ambition is to contribute to the advancement of the maritime and aviation sectors by delivering innovative, efficient, and sustainable transport solutions while creating meaningful value for the industry and society.
